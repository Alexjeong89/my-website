Product ManagerOne of the first NFT games.

I spent over two years as a product manager at CryptoKitties, one of the first NFT games. The community was passionate, and the big drops brought huge spikes in traffic. A lot of my work came down to two questions: how do we keep players excited between those big moments, and how do we make sure nothing breaks when everyone shows up at once?

For the first, we tried seasonal challenges, limited-edition collectibles and small bits of in-app storytelling. Players responded well, and engagement went up about 22%. I also worked with creators and influencers on community-focused launches, which brought in over $800K in marketplace activity in three months.

For the second, I spent a lot of time getting support, comms and product on the same page before high-traffic events. We also set up a shared QA process across teams, which cut post-launch issues by 35% and made shipping less stressful. Over my time there, we released more than 10 updates, many shaped directly by what players told us.
